Objective: "How does deprivation relate to healthy life expectancy across Birmingham wards?”

Analysed public health datasets from BCO, ONS, and OHID sources. 
Used three-year pooled healthy life expectancy estimates to improve reliability of ward-level comparisons 
and reduce random variation in small-area health data. Used Power Query to filter and transform the dataset, 
ensuring reproducibility and consistency in the analysis workflow.

Output: The analysis demonstrates a clear relationship between deprivation and life expectancy across Birmingham wards. 
More deprived wards generally experience lower life expectancy, highlighting the impact of wider social determinants of health.
Built Interactive Power BI dashboard to present KPIs. Recommendations supported by data visualization and statistical 
interpretation.
